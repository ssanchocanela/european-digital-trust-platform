# Ecosystem Overview

**Status:** [PRODUCT] / [EXPERIMENTAL]

## Three connected identity domains

### Natural person — EUDI Wallet

Answers questions such as: **Who is this person? What trusted attributes can they prove?**

### Organisation — European Business Wallet

Answers: **What organisation is this? Who can act for it? What credentials, rights and trusted communications belong to it?**

### Product — Digital Product Passport

Answers: **What product is this? Who is responsible for it? What trusted sustainability, compliance and lifecycle information is associated with it?**

## Key convergence

The Business Wallet is the natural orchestration point between organisational identity, human representation and product identity:

```text
EUDI Wallet (person)
       |
       | representation / mandate
       v
Business Wallet (organisation) ---- QERDS / trust services
       |
       | manages / is responsible for
       v
Digital Product Passport (product)
       |
       v
DPP Registry + EO/service-provider hosted DPP data
```

This convergence is a central hypothesis to validate.
