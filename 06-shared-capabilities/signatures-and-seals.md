# Signatures and Seals

Track QES, QSeal, QSCD/remote signing integration and the different roles of natural-person signatures and legal-person seals across EUDI, EUBW and DPP.

**Status:** [REGULATORY] trust-service concepts; [PRODUCT] orchestration

- QES expresses a natural person's signature; QSeal supports origin/integrity evidence for a legal person. They are not interchangeable with authentication or representation.
- The platform may orchestrate document preparation, approval, wallet invocation, QTSP calls and evidence collection.
- Qualified keys, devices/services, certificates and provider obligations remain inside the applicable qualified-service boundary.
- Mandate policy must be evaluated before a person signs or triggers a seal for an organisation.

`[OPEN]` Requires validation: target QTSPs, CSC/RQES profiles, local versus remote signing, signature formats, long-term validation and evidence retention.
