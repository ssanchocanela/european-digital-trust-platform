# EUDI Wallet Overview

**Status:** [REGULATORY] / [SPECIFICATION]

The EUDI Wallet provides a secure, user-controlled environment for natural persons to manage PID and electronic attestations, present attributes to Relying Parties and use qualified electronic signatures/seals where applicable.

For this product exploration, EUDI Wallet is both:

1. a source of reusable architectural and protocol foundations; and
2. an external ecosystem with which the Business Wallet and EUDI Services must interoperate.

ARF 3.0 explicitly focuses the EUDI Wallet on natural persons and notes that legal-person Wallet Units have been removed in view of the separate Business Wallet development.
