# Mandates and Representation

**Status:** [PROPOSED-REGULATORY] / [OPEN]

Representation is a key differentiator between personal and organisational identity.

The Business Wallet should support scenarios where:

- multiple natural persons act for one organisation;
- one person represents multiple organisations;
- rights are limited by role, mandate, purpose or time;
- mandates can be issued, verified, tracked and revoked;
- an EUDI Wallet may be used to identify the natural person acting for the organisation.

The relationship with the EU Digital Power of Attorney and national representation sources requires dedicated analysis.
