# Digital Product Passport

**Status:** [REGULATORY] / [SPECIFICATION] / [PRODUCT]

The Digital Product Passport (DPP) introduces trusted digital identity and structured information for products.

A DPP is a structured collection of product data shared through a unique identifier and accessible electronically through a data carrier such as a QR code.

For this project, DPP is particularly important because it connects **product identity** with the **Economic Operator responsible for the product**.
