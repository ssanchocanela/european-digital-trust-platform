# Business Wallet Regulatory Framework

**Status:** [PROPOSED-REGULATORY]

The European Business Wallet framework is still evolving. All requirements derived from the Commission proposal or supporting documents must therefore be explicitly identified as proposed rather than final.

This document will track:

- Regulation proposal evolution;
- implementing/delegated acts when available;
- relationship with eIDAS/EUDI;
- obligations for Economic Operators and Public Sector Bodies;
- provider requirements;
- QERDS and trust services;
- interoperability requirements.
