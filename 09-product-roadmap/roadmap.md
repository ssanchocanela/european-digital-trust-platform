# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Knowledge baseline

Regulatory/specification repository, capability map and architecture hypotheses. Exit: evidence policy applied, sources versioned, open questions assigned.

## Phase 2 — Reuse & gap assessment

Pin official RI and EUDIPLO releases; create feature/TS traceability, threat model, SBOM/license review and adapter contract spike. Exit: adopt/reject decision for each runtime component.

## Phase 3 — EUDI Services MVP

Issuer and verifier managed-service PoCs for one bounded public-body/SME use case. Exit: cross-wallet and conformance tests, tenant isolation evidence, lifecycle/status, operational runbook and data-minimization review.

## Phase 4 — Business Wallet foundation

Organisation workspace, multi-user administration, authoritative mandate integration, credentials and one QTSP-backed QES/QSeal flow. Exit depends on the then-current EUBW legal baseline.

## Phase 5 — QERDS + DPP modules

One qualified communication/provider integration and one product-group DPP workflow, keeping Registry and data hosting separate. Exit: legal/evidence validation and production interface confirmation.

Security, privacy, interoperability and legislative tracking are continuous workstreams rather than a final phase.
