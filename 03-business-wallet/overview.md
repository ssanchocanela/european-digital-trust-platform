# European Business Wallet

**Status:** [PROPOSED-REGULATORY] / [PRODUCT]

The European Business Wallet initiative targets the specific needs of Economic Operators and Public Sector Bodies in B2G/G2B and B2B digital interactions.

The Commission's supporting analysis identifies needs including organisational identification, business attributes, mandates/representation, legally valid communication, traceability, integration with business systems and interoperability with EUDI Wallets.

## Product hypothesis

Build a Business Wallet as a **digital trust workspace for organisations**, not merely as a credential container.
