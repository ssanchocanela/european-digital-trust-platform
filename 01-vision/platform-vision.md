# Platform Vision

**Status:** [PRODUCT] / [EXPERIMENTAL]

## Product thesis

European digital identity is evolving from a citizen-wallet problem into a broader trust infrastructure connecting **natural persons, organisations, representatives, products, credentials and transactions**.

The opportunity is to build a reusable **European Digital Trust Platform** rather than isolated point solutions.

## Product tracks

### Business Wallet

A digital trust workspace for an Economic Operator or Public Sector Body. Candidate capabilities include:

- organisational identification and authentication;
- business credentials and electronic attestations;
- mandates, roles and representation;
- qualified electronic signatures and seals;
- legally valid communication / QERDS;
- auditability and traceability;
- Digital Product Passport management;
- integration with ERP/CRM and business applications;
- interoperability with EUDI Wallets.

### EUDI Services

A managed participation layer for organisations that need to issue to or verify EUDI Wallets but do not want to implement the ecosystem themselves.

Initial services:

- Issuance as a Service;
- Verification as a Service;
- trust resolution;
- credential lifecycle;
- issuer/RP onboarding support;
- integration APIs and SDKs.

## Design principle

The customer-facing experience should abstract protocol and trust complexity while preserving interoperability and compliance with the European ecosystem.
