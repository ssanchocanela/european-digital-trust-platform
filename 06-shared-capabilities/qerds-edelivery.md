# QERDS / eDelivery

Explore a reusable legally-valid communication capability for the Business Wallet, keeping QERDS qualification/service obligations separate from the eDelivery interoperability technology used underneath.

**Status:** [REGULATORY] QERDS; [SPECIFICATION] eDelivery; [PRODUCT] integration hypothesis

Candidate functions include addressing, participant discovery, submission/receipt evidence, reliable delivery, encryption/signing, conversation state and retention. A platform adapter may connect to a qualified registered delivery provider and, where selected by the network, an eDelivery AS4 Access Point with SMP/SML discovery.

The Commission provides eDelivery specifications, conformance testing and reusable/sample implementations. `[OPEN]` Requires validation: whether a target network uses AS4, its profile version, discovery and trust model, and how technical receipts map to qualified-service evidence.
