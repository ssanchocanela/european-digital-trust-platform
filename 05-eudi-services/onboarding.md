# Issuer and Relying Party Onboarding

**Status:** [PRODUCT] / [OPEN]

The platform should reduce technical and ecosystem onboarding complexity for issuers and Relying Parties while keeping their roles and configurations separate.

Shared onboarding concerns include organisation/tenant administration, environment promotion, service identifiers, trust sources, certificates, registration/notification, key custody, contacts, policy ownership, interoperability testing and evidence of approval.

Issuer-specific onboarding additionally configures Authentic Sources, Connectors, Credential Types, policies, Delegation Profiles and lifecycle responsibilities. See [issuer onboarding](issuer-onboarding.md).

`[OPEN]` Registration, notification, certificate and legal-role requirements vary by attestation type, scheme and environment. Technical onboarding completion must not be represented as regulatory authorization.
