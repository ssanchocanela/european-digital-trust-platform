# Economic Operator Onboarding to the DPP Registry

**Status:** [SPECIFICATION] / [PRODUCT]

The current DPP Registry requires an organisation to be verified before registering/managing DPPs.

The current enrolment flow uses EU Login and an EC-sealed PDF declaration. The declaration is countersigned using:

- a **Qualified Electronic Seal (QSeal)** for a legal-person Economic Operator; or
- a **Qualified Electronic Signature (QES)** for a natural-person Economic Operator/sole trader.

This is an important integration opportunity for the future Business Wallet: organisational identity, representation and QSeal/QES could potentially streamline this journey when supported by the applicable DPP framework.
