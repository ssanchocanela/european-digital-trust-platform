# Product Tracks

## Track A — Business Wallet + DPP

Explore a Business Wallet product that combines candidate EUBW capabilities with value-added DPP management and enterprise integration. `[PROPOSED-REGULATORY]` The Commission proposal is not final law; no proposed feature is treated as an adopted requirement.

## Track B — EUDI Issuance & Verification Services

Provide simple managed services for public bodies and companies to participate as issuers and relying parties in the EUDI ecosystem.

## Shared foundation

Both tracks should reuse common capabilities for trust resolution, cryptography, credentials, registration, audit, integration and policy management.
