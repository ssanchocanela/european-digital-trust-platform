# EUDI Services

**Status:** [PRODUCT]

Many organisations will need to issue credentials to EUDI Wallets or request and verify attributes without implementing the complete EUDI trust and protocol stack.

The product proposition is to expose simple integration services while the platform handles European interoperability complexity.

Issuance supports configurable Gateway, Managed Issuance and Hybrid models. A Delegation Profile assigns source retrieval, eligibility, business rules, approval and lifecycle execution to the customer, platform or a joint workflow; a Provider Operating Profile constrains those assignments for EAA, PuB-EAA or QEAA and binds the legal provider, trust identity and key operator. External Authentic Sources remain authoritative for source data.

See [Issuance as a Service](issuance-as-a-service.md), the [issuer product model](issuer-product-model.md), and [Verification as a Service](verification-as-a-service.md).
